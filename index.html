Our Story

How Everything Started

Almeida General Construction combines over 18 years of woodworking experience to build quality cabinets, furniture, and other custom wood elements for your home or business. We carefully build custom cabinets, staircases, decks, bars, and other fine wood elements. We strive to meet and exceed our customers’ expectations at all times.

With thousands of projects completed in New England, you can be assured we have references for every type of project.

Isaias Almeida

Owner

“Izzy” has over 20 years of experience in the industry. He oversees the construction activities and is heavily involved in planning, organizing, and controlling the projects to make sure the clients are 100% satisfied.

Custom Woodwork

A design team is available to help you find the right design for your project. Cabinets are custom-designed to fit your needs and budget, with professional installation by our team.

Residential Construction

From the first idea through design and the final build. We have experience getting the necessary permits and making the process easier for the homeowner.

Commercial Construction

As a general contractor, we take on commercial work at every scale, from small projects to larger ones, and stay with the job until it meets the client’s needs.
